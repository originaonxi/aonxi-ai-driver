The product difference

AI does the driving. People keep the judgment.

Traditional copilots wait for a human to click through a task. Aonxi starts with the outcome and keeps working until the KPI is met or a person needs to decide.

Traditional co-pilot

  • Input: “Draft this email.”
  • Human: researches, chooses tools, executes, checks and follows up.
  • AI: suggests a next step inside one app.
  • Result: more software; the work still sits with the operator.

Aonxi AI Driver

  • Input: “Book 10 qualified meetings this month.”
  • AI: plans, researches, enriches, writes, sends, monitors and iterates.
  • Human: approves spend, sensitive outreach and exceptions.
  • Result: an outcome, a KPI report, evidence and a cost trail.
